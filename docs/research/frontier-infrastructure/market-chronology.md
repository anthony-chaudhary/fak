independently sourced evidence. Keep the historical entry, mark its outcome, and never silently
promote it to fact.

## Acquisition, failure, and cancellation watchlist

The public ledger remains much stronger on launches and funding than on failures. Every refresh
must search for the following states, not just positive announcements:

- acquired, acquihired, technology licensed, assets sold, merged, renamed;
- shutdown, bankruptcy, restructuring, layoffs, impaired assets, covenant breach;
- delayed, canceled, downsized, phased, customer terminated, grid interconnect lost;
- product preview vs general availability vs deprecated/end-of-life;
- customer concentration, take-or-pay renegotiation, unpaid capacity, or stranded site;
- security, reliability, or performance incident that changes real goodput.

Priority named watches from [`startups-landscape.md`](startups-landscape.md): CoreWeave,
Nebius, Crusoe, Lambda, Together AI, Fluidstack, Nscale, Groq, Cerebras, SambaNova, Etched,
d-Matrix, Tenstorrent, Positron, inference/runtime vendors, KV-cache/storage vendors, optical
and cooling startups, and generation/datacenter developers.

## Event-state rules

1. Funding is evidence of financing, not technical capability, adoption, profitability, or
   deployed capacity.
2. Partnership is evidence of intent/contract only to the disclosed boundary; name launch state.
3. “Production” requires a defined customer/workload and availability boundary, not a press title.
4. Acquisition talks remain rumor until signed and remain incomplete until closing.
5. License, talent transfer, and partnership are not synonymous with acquisition.
6. Product retirement, cancellation, and failure remain in the chronology; do not delete them.
7. Repeated articles based on one source are one rumor origin, not corroboration.
